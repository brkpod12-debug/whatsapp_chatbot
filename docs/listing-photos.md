# Listing photographs

The listing photographs on this site are stand-ins, not pictures of the actual
flats. They are here so the cards and property pages do not fall back to random
picsum imagery while the sellers' own photographs are collected.

**Replace them.** Drag the real photographs onto each listing in Studio
(`/studio` > Properties > the listing > Photos & video). Uploading a Main photo
replaces the stand-in; the seed scripts never overwrite a listing that already has one.

Until then, every property page carries the caption held on the Property page
singleton (`imageNote`): "Representative photograph. Pictures of this flat are
shared privately on enquiry." Do not delete that line while stand-ins are up.
