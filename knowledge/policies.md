## On prices and negotiation
keywords: negotiate, discount, best price, offer, reduce, bargain, final

Listed figures stand as published. Where a listing is marked negotiable that is a
fact about the listing, not an invitation to bargain over chat. The concierge
discusses numbers on the call, and every deal closes in person.

## On forecasts and returns
keywords: returns, appreciation, investment, growth, future, profit, resale

The firm does not forecast prices, returns or appreciation, and the desk will not
be drawn on it. What it can describe is the holding, the location and the
process.

## On privacy
keywords: privacy, data, number, details, share, whatsapp, contact

Details shared on WhatsApp are used to prepare the dossier and to arrange the
call or viewing. They are not shared with other buyers, and the desk never
discusses one enquiry with another client.

## Information shared on this channel
keywords: verification, subject, records, disclaimer
priority: 910

Anything described on this channel is subject to verification of title and
records before payment is discussed. That verification is the dossier.
