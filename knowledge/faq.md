## What the private dossier contains
keywords: dossier, brochure, documents, papers, details, report

The dossier is prepared per holding and shared before any payment is discussed.
It carries the chain-of-title audit run by independent counsel, the survey and
measurement record, the approval position, the itemised cost sheet, and the
photography or drone pass for the holding.

## How title is verified
keywords: title, legal, verification, encumbrance, dispute, counsel, papers

Independent counsel runs the full chain-of-title audit on every holding, and the
buyer receives it in the dossier before any payment is discussed. The desk does
not certify the title of a specific property in conversation; counsel does, in
writing.

## Whether a buyer based abroad can purchase
keywords: nri, abroad, foreign, overseas, usa, uae, remote, fema

The firm works with buyers based abroad and can run viewings by video and drone
pass. Eligibility, FEMA and taxation depend on the individual and the asset, so
the concierge answers those directly rather than the desk.

## Whether a non-farmer can buy agricultural land
keywords: agricultural, farmer, eligible, eligibility, farmland, permission

This is a legal question, not a desk question. The concierge will take it, with
counsel where needed. The desk does not answer eligibility either way.

## What the quoted price includes
keywords: price, cost, includes, extra, charges, registration, stamp, duty

The quoted figure is for the holding itself. Registration, stamp duty, survey and
any fencing or development charges are itemised separately on the cost sheet in
the dossier, so the total is never a surprise. The concierge walks through the
sheet on the call.

## How farmland is measured
keywords: acres, guntas, measurement, area, survey, extent, size
scope: asset_class=farmland

Farmland is quoted in acres, with the survey record in the dossier. Plot-level
extents are confirmed by the survey rather than by the listing, and the desk
quotes only the extent published for the holding.

## How to get started
keywords: start, begin, next, book, call, appointment, viewing

Two ways in. Request the private dossier for a holding, or book a thirty-minute
first call with the concierge. Both go to the same place: the concierge takes it
from there.
