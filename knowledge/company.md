## Who Josh Properties is

Josh Properties is a private real-estate advisory at Road No. 12, Banjara Hills,
Hyderabad, operating since 2017 under RERA registration P02400005461. The firm
curates villas, apartments and title-verified farmland across Hyderabad and
Telangana. It holds a deliberately small register rather than a large listing
book, and every client works with one concierge from first call to registration.

## How the firm works
keywords: process, steps, procedure, stages, timeline, registration
priority: 930

Five stages. First call on day zero. Private viewing between day three and day
seven. Title and survey in week two. Offer and dossier in week three. Booking and
registration between weeks four and six. Numbers are discussed on the call with
the concierge, never over chat.

## What the desk assistant can and cannot do
keywords: bot, assistant, human, concierge, speak, talk, agent
priority: 920

The desk assistant answers questions about the register, the process and the
firm, and arranges a first call or a private viewing. It does not negotiate,
quote a figure it was not given, or advise on law, tax or eligibility. Anything
of that kind goes to the concierge, who calls back.
